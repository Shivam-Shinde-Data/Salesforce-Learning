# Day 6: Salesforce Editions, Org Types & User Interface

## What is a Salesforce Org?

A Salesforce Org (Organization) is a Salesforce environment that contains an organization's users, data, security settings, objects, reports, dashboards, and automations.

Each company using Salesforce typically has its own Org.

### Example

ABC Pvt Ltd and XYZ Technologies may both use Salesforce, but each has a separate Org with its own data and configurations.

---

## Salesforce Editions

Salesforce provides different editions based on business requirements and budget.

### Starter Edition

Designed for small businesses and startups.

Features:

- Basic CRM functionality
- Lead management
- Contact management

---

### Professional Edition

Suitable for growing businesses.

Features:

- Enhanced reporting
- Additional automation
- More customization

---

### Enterprise Edition

Most commonly used edition in organizations.

Features:

- Advanced automation
- API access
- Custom development capabilities
- Advanced security controls

---

### Unlimited Edition

Provides the highest level of functionality, storage, and support.

Typically used by large enterprises.

---

## Salesforce Environments

Organizations usually work with multiple Salesforce environments.

### Production Org

Production is the live environment where actual users perform business operations.

Characteristics:

- Contains real business data
- Used by end users
- Supports daily business activities

---

### Sandbox

A Sandbox is a testing environment that is typically copied from Production.

Characteristics:

- Safe for testing
- Used for development and validation
- Prevents accidental impact on business operations

---

### Developer Org

A Developer Org is a free Salesforce environment designed for learning and practice.

Characteristics:

- Free to use
- Ideal for beginners
- Supports experimentation without affecting real business data

---

## What is Trailhead?

Trailhead is Salesforce's official learning platform.

It provides structured learning paths for:

- Salesforce Administrators
- Developers
- Consultants
- Architects

Users earn badges and complete trails as they progress.

---

## Trailhead Components

### Badges

Awarded for completing individual learning modules.

### Trails

Collections of related learning modules.

### Superbadges

Advanced hands-on challenges that test practical Salesforce skills.

---

## Salesforce User Interface (Lightning Experience)

Lightning Experience is the modern Salesforce user interface used by most organizations.

---

## App Launcher

The App Launcher allows users to access Salesforce applications such as:

- Sales App
- Service App
- Marketing App

It functions similarly to a start menu.

---

## Navigation Bar

The Navigation Bar provides access to commonly used tabs such as:

- Accounts
- Contacts
- Leads
- Opportunities

---

## Global Search

Global Search allows users to search records across Salesforce from a single search box.

Examples:

- Accounts
- Contacts
- Opportunities
- Cases

---

## Record Page

A Record Page displays detailed information about a specific record.

Example:

Contact Record

- Name
- Phone
- Email
- Related Activities

---

## Production vs Sandbox vs Developer Org

| Feature | Production | Sandbox | Developer Org |
|----------|------------|----------|--------------|
| Real Data | Yes | Usually Copied | No |
| Testing | Limited | Yes | Yes |
| Learning | No | Sometimes | Yes |
| Business Usage | Yes | No | No |
| Free | No | Included with Editions | Yes |

---

## Key Takeaways

- A Salesforce Org is an organization's Salesforce environment.
- Salesforce offers multiple editions for different business needs.
- Enterprise Edition is the most commonly used edition.
- Production contains real business data.
- Sandbox is used for testing and development.
- Developer Org is used for learning and practice.
- Trailhead is Salesforce's official learning platform.
- Lightning Experience is the modern Salesforce interface.

# Interview Questions and Answers

## Q1. What is a Salesforce Org?

**Answer:**

A Salesforce Org is a Salesforce environment that contains an organization's users, data, security settings, objects, reports, dashboards, and automations.

---

## Q2. What is the difference between Production and Sandbox?

**Answer:**

Production is the live environment containing real business data and users, while Sandbox is a separate testing environment used for development, testing, and validation before deployment.

---

## Q3. Why should changes be tested in Sandbox first?

**Answer:**

Testing in Sandbox prevents accidental disruption of business operations and allows administrators to validate configurations safely before deploying them to Production.

---

## Q4. What is a Developer Org?

**Answer:**

A Developer Org is a free Salesforce environment provided for learning, experimentation, and practice without affecting real business data.

---

## Q5. What is Trailhead?

**Answer:**

Trailhead is Salesforce's official online learning platform that provides modules, trails, badges, and hands-on exercises for learning Salesforce skills.

---

## Q6. Which Salesforce Edition is most commonly used in organizations?

**Answer:**

Enterprise Edition is the most commonly used Salesforce edition because it provides advanced customization, automation, API access, and security features.
