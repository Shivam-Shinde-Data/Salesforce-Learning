# Day 36: Reports in Salesforce

## What is a Report?

A Report is:

```text
A list of records organized and summarized to provide business insights.
```

Reports help users analyze data and make decisions

---

# Report Components

- Report Type
- Filters
- Columns
- Grouping

---

# Types of Reports

| Report Type | Description |
|---|---|
| Tabular | Simple table |
| Summary | Grouped data |
| Matrix | Rows and Columns grouping |
| Joined | Multiple reports together |

---

# Example

Patient Report:

| Patient | Department |
|---|---|
| Rahul | Cardiology |
| Priya | Neurology |

Summary Report:

Cardiology = 20

Neurology = 15

# Reports vs Dashboards

| Reports | Dashboards |
|---|---|
| Detailed Data | Visual Charts |
| Tables | Graphs |
| Source of Dashboard | Visualization Layer |

---

# Key Takeaways

- Reports organize business data.
- Reports use filters and grouping.
- Summary Reports are most commonly used.
- Dashboards use Reports as a data source.
- Reports are essential for business analysis.
